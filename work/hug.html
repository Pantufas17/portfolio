This project was carried out as part of a one-month integration course. Working directly with the Geneva University Hospitals (HUG), the goal was to create a web platform that communicates the importance of blood donation and encourages people to take action.

The challenge was not just technical — it required understanding the public health communication context, the target audience, and the sensitivities around the topic.
