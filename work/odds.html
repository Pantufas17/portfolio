ODDS

01 — The Problem

Why organ donation
needs a better experience.

Switzerland has one of the lowest organ donation rates in Europe. The administrative barriers, lack of clarity, and emotional sensitivity around the topic make it difficult for people to make an informed decision or take action.

The goal of ODDS was to rethink this experience — making it more accessible, human, and trustworthy through a thoughtful digital platform.
